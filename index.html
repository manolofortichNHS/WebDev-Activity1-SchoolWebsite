DEPED CURRICULUMS

The DepEd curriculum includes the Revised K to 10 Curriculum (MATATAG), the Senior High School Curriculum, and Special Curricular Programs, all officially provided through DepEd’s curriculum guides and learning resources.

Junior High School (JHS)

The MATATAG curriculum strengthens foundational skills in reading, mathematics, and life skills. It provides updated curriculum guides for all subjects from Grade 7 to Grade 10.

Senior High School (SHS)

DepEd will implement the Strengthened Senior High School (SSHS) Curriculum starting SY 2026–2027

Special Curricular Programs (SCPs)

DepEd also offers specialized programs for learners with specific interests or talents.
